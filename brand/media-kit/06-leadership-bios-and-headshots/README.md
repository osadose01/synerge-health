# Leadership Bios & Headshots

Place approved executive and clinical advisory portraits in this directory before distributing to external press, panels, or event organisers.

## Photography Specifications
- **Format:** High-resolution `.jpg` or `.png`, minimum `1600px` wide (supply both **1:1 square** / **4:5 portrait** and **16:9 landscape** crops).
- **Lighting & Framing:** Natural light or studio backdrop with head and shoulders comfortably framed; follow `BRAND-GUIDELINES.md` (authentic real photography — no AI-generated headshots).
- **Consent:** Confirm written permission from every pictured founder, clinician, or advisor prior to publication.
