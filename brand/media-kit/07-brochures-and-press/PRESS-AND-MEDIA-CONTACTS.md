## 3. Editorial & Attribution Guidelines for Journalists & Partners

1. **Naming:** Refer to the parent organisation as **Synerge Health** on first mention (`Africa's healthtech venture studio`). Do not write *"Synerge Healthcare"* or *"Synergy Health"*.
2. **Venture Attribution:** Refer to studio ventures using **`from Synerge Health`** (e.g., *Metis, from Synerge Health*) or **`A Synerge Health venture`**.
3. **Clinical & Product Claims:** Describe verifiable capabilities (offline-first pharmacy checkout, FEFO batch enforcement, NAFDAC/PCN alignment, `2°C–8°C` cold-chain telemetry, Net 30 wholesale trade reconciliation). Avoid unverified outcome guarantees.
4. **Confidentiality:** Do not publish patient-identifying information or unapproved partner/pilot names.
